Harvest material enters the books

Harvest

harvestorigin

One form per truck load at the farm. The worksheet follows the crew's notebook: license → strain box → bins. Scanning a plant tag starts a strain box and fills the strain from Canix plant batches.

On the form

  • Date, Fresh Frozen checkbox, truck # (optional)
  • License Plate (trucks table + past plates) and Driver (drivers table), both required
  • Per strain box: Source UID, strain, location, Transfer UID
  • Per bin: wet weight (required), plant count (once per strain is enough)

Math

plantCount  = Σ plant count
binCount    = bins with weight > 0
wetWeightLb = Σ bin wet weight
lb / plant  = wetWeightLb ÷ plantCount

Then

  • Split into one record per Source UID on submit
  • Picture + CSV of the load to send on WhatsApp
  • Adds wet_whole_plant to the ledger
  • Labor goes in the Labor Log, not here

Fresh Frozen

fresh_frozenorigin

A harvest picked with Fresh Frozen ticked doesn't dry: it's bagged on the Fresh Frozen form and goes into totes in the freezer for extraction.

On the form

  • Site, strain, bag count, total lbs, tote count
  • Freezer, freezer temp °F, intended use

Math

lb / bag = totalLb ÷ bagCount

Then

  • Into the freezer: Raw Inventory → Fresh Frozen tab
  • A Wet Intake to a freezer location also counts as fresh frozen
  • Ships on a Product Transfer as type 6

Drying intake, dry room, take down

Harvest Intake — Wet

intake_wetMetrc Queue

One form per package at our door. The crew picks the package from what's on its way to us in Canix (or scans it if it isn't listed) and weighs its bins; Metrc paperwork is filled in later by the Canix sync.

On the form

  • Top: package (dropdown of incoming packages from Canix, or Not listed → scan / last 5). Strain fills from Canix and is locked
  • Truck #: automatic per day — first manifest is Truck 1, the next new one Truck 2, across all farms; packages on the same manifest share it. A package with no manifest in Canix is put on a truck by hand
  • Weighmaster (dropdown of the facility's certified weighmasters), drying / freezer location
  • Bottom: rows of # bins, tare (Macro-Bin 55.5 lb, Cardboard Box 16.5 lb, or Other — saved for next time), weight
  • Manifest photo — optional, only for a paper manifest
  • Not asked: farm license, CID, manifest #, driver (the Canix sync fills those, every 3 h); person completing (whoever submits); project (intake labor stays facility cost); Fresh Frozen (from the location)

Math

totalTareLb = Σ (bins × tare)
totalWetLb  = Σ (weight − bins × tare)
binCount    = Σ bins

Then

  • Freezer location → Fresh Frozen tab; otherwise → Drying
  • Shows Waiting for Metrc sync until Canix fills it, and Check strain when Canix's strain differs (aliases count as a match)
  • Incoming transfer must be accepted in Metrc within 24 h of departure. Urgent at ≤ 8 h left
  • Metrc adjustment (yes / lbs) is added afterwards from Harvest Intakes (Edit) — not on the form at the door

Drying

state, no form

Currently drying means a submitted Wet Intake with no Take Down for its UID. UIDs match when one ends with the other (4+ characters). Fresh-frozen intakes never count. Material hangs about 5–10 days; anything needing more dry time just isn't taken down yet.

Take Down — Dry

dry_checkMetrc Queuemoisture loss

Boxes the dried material under a new package UID (New Dry UID). Pick the room, then the package from what's still drying there; it pulls strain, farm license, CID and wet weight from the intake.

On the form

  • Drying room, package (shows its Intake UID), New Dry UID
  • Boxes worksheet: box # (numbers itself), weight (net — the scale tares the box)
  • Release: to bucking (default), hold for quality, or hold for an order

Math

dryWeightLb     = Σ (box weight)
moistureLossLb  = wetIntakeLb − dryWeightLb
moistureLossPct = moistureLossLb ÷ wetIntakeLb

Then

  • Several farm packages of one strain can merge into one Dry UID; their dry weights add up and they become one lot
  • The farm UID is aliased to the Dry UID so yields stay one lot
  • Raw Inventory → On-Stem

Processing bucking and trimming

On-Stem Flower: holds and the bucking queue

buck_hold.html

Held Take Downs wait here until a manager with buck release taps Release to bucking: All of it, or Part for an order (split it in Metrc under a new tag first, then enter the lbs and new UID; lbs must be less than what's left). Managers also set the bucking queue order.

unbuckedLb (on-stem tag) = dry weight − parts released
remaining in queue       = starting weight − boxes pulled on open Bucking pages

Bucking

buckMetrc Queueconserving

One page per on-stem UID, open across days (day tabs) and shared by several tablets, whose rows merge. Picking the package fills UID, strain and dry unbucked weight. No new Metrc tag unless the lot is ticked Holding as bucked flower.

On the form (running logs)

  • Boxes taken off the dry weight (lbs)
  • Bucked flower by person (employee # or name, lbs)
  • Recovered from leaf (lbs, nobody's)
  • Trim weighed during the batch
  • Close-out: stems log, mold, floor waste, Calculate

Math

startingDryLb  = Σ boxes
dryLeftLb      = take-down weight − Σ boxes
buckedFlowerLb = Σ bucked + Σ recovered
totalOutputLb  = bucked + trim + stems
                 + mold + floor waste
lossLb         = startingDryLb − totalOutputLb
lossPct        = lossLb ÷ startingDryLb
buckYieldPct   = buckedFlowerLb ÷ startingDryLb
Calculate: with 2 of stems / mold / floor waste
weighed, the 3rd = boxes − everything else (loss → 0)

Then

  • Bucked flower → Raw Inventory Bucked (the trimming queue), under the on-stem tag, or newBuckedUid from the Metrc Queue when held
  • Bucking trim → Trim tab, later into a Mixed Trim Package
  • Stems → stems; mold and floor waste → waste
  • Recovered-from-leaf counts in yield, not in per-person pounds

Machine Trim

machine_trimconserving

Bucked flower through the Mobius and sorters, splitting A-buds from smalls. Often the machine is only the first pass: the lot then goes on to Hand Trim (with Machine ticked) for a hand finish. Otherwise its outputs go straight to flower, smalls, trim and waste.

On the form

  • Bucked flower UID, strain, machine, trim style
  • Input bucked lbs; outputs: A-buds, smalls, machine shake, sugar trim, waste
  • Machine run hours, crew

Math

totalOutputLb = A + smalls + shake + sugar + waste
varianceLb    = totalOutputLb − inputBuckedLb
lossPct       = (input − totalOutput) ÷ input
aBudPct       = flowerALb ÷ inputBuckedLb
lbPerHour     = inputBuckedLb ÷ runHours

Then

  • Machine only: flower (A) → Flower, smalls (B) → Smalls, shake and sugar trim → Trim, waste
  • Machine + hand finish: the machined lot goes to Hand Trim, which records the final flower, smalls, trim and waste

Hand Trim / Hand Touch

hand_trimMetrc Queueconserving

The trimming work order: one page per lot, open across days, picked from every bucked lot (fills strain, CID and starting bucked weight, traced back through Take Down to Wet Intake).

On the form

  • Lot, strain, grade, CID, starting bucked lbs, Machine / Hand
  • Trimmer Rate $/lb (required to submit)
  • Weighing worksheet: employee # and grams per bag (Bags × 454 + a partial)
  • End of run: smalls, Normal trim, A+ trim, waste, bag count

Math

totalGrams       = Σ bag grams
finishedFlowerLb = totalGrams ÷ 454
gramsPerTrimmer  = totalGrams ÷ distinct employees
totalOutputLb    = flower + smalls + normal trim
                   + A+ trim + sugar trim + waste
varianceLb       = totalOutputLb − startingBuckedLb
lossPct          = (start − totalOutput) ÷ start
trimmer pay      = pounds × trimRatePerLb

Then

  • Weight sign-off: in the Metrc Queue it shows Waiting for weight validation until a manager (Metrc Queue → Confirm weights) checks the weights and taps Confirm weights; then Weight confirmed. Until then its tags can't go in and it can't be marked Done. Changing a weight afterwards (Fix form) puts it back to waiting
  • Metrc Queue asks for the new tags: newFinishedUid (flower) and newSmallsUid
  • Trim gets no per-strain tag; it waits on the lot tag for a Mixed Trim Package
  • Waste stays on the source tag and is wasted out there
  • Raw Inventory: Flower, Smalls, Trim

Smalls Hand Trim

smalls_trimconserving

The paper "Work Order Form (Smalls | By the Hourly)": one page per smalls package, open across days (a tab per day) until it's submitted, like Hand Trim, with a daily employee × flavor tally (lbs) under the form.

On the form

  • Project; package picked from Raw Inventory's Smalls (fills strain, starting lbs)
  • Worksheet: employee #, bags × grams each, stamped with the day
  • End of run: shake, waste, new UID for finished smalls, back-of-form initials, team lead, labor hours

Math

finishedSmallsLb = Σ grams ÷ 454
totalOutputLb    = finished + shake + waste
varianceLb       = totalOutputLb − startingLb
lossPct          = (start − totalOutput) ÷ start

Then

  • Takes its input off Smalls in Raw Inventory
  • Finished smalls listed under its new UID; shake → Trim

Mixed Trim Package

trim_packageMetrc Queue

Every couple of days the trim on hand (Hand Trim's Normal / A+, Bucking trim, shake) is packaged together instead of one package per strain.

On the form

  • Made by, package type (Trim / A+ / Sugar / Shake, mixed)
  • Per source package: UID (strain from Canix), trim type, lbs

Math

totalLb      = Σ row lbs
packageCount = distinct source UIDs

Then

  • One new tag, newMixedTrimUid, from the Metrc Queue
  • Each row comes off its source package's trim; the mixed package shows under its new tag, strain "Mixed"

Manufacturing material becomes product

Pre-Roll Production

preroll_productionMetrc Queuetransform

One page per run, started from the Pre-Roll & Ground Flower Queue (a Sheet request PR# or a hand-added PQ-#). Open across days until the operator submits it at the end of the run; the second-person count check happens after that, in the Metrc Queue.

On the form

  • Production Request (fills Batch ID, brand, pack type, strain, source UID, size)
  • Starting weight (g), input category, pre-roll size
  • Output log per day: pre-rolls, waste g
  • Crew check against the Master Schedule
  • End of run: new UIDs, finish date, QC (optional)

Math

startingLb    = startingWtG ÷ 454
totalPrerolls = Σ count
totalWasteG   = Σ waste g
accountedPct  = (prerolls × size + waste g)
                ÷ startingWtG       ~100%
pre-rolls due = units × pack size
labor $ / pre-roll = run's Labor Log share
                     ÷ totalPrerolls

Then

  • Input leaves the biomass ledger as finished goods (transform, not loss)
  • Takes the input off Raw Inventory by UID and category
  • Work order counts as done when a submitted run carries its ref
  • Count verification: in the Metrc Queue it shows Needs count verification until a second person (Metrc Queue → Confirm weights / verify counts) checks the pre-roll and waste totals and taps Verify counts; until then it can't be marked Done. Changing a count afterwards (Fix form) puts it back

Ground Flower Pouches

pouch_productionMetrc Queuetransform

Pre-Roll Production for ground flower pouches (Howie Roll 14 g for now). Shares the pre-roll queue; a request whose name says "Ground" is pouches.

On the form

  • Material Going In: material (flower, smalls, trim A / A+ / Normal), source UID, grams
  • Pouch size (starts at 14 g)
  • Pouch Output log: pouches, waste g
  • End of run: newPouchUid, newWasteUid, QC (optional) — the operator submits without a verifier

Math

startingWtG  = Σ input grams
startingLb   = startingWtG ÷ 454
totalPouches = Σ pouches
accountedPct = (pouches × 14 + waste g)
               ÷ startingWtG
flagged outside 95%–105%

Then

  • Each input row comes off its own UID and category in Raw Inventory
  • Count verification: shows Needs count verification in the Metrc Queue until a second person checks the pouch and waste totals and taps Verify counts; until then it can't be marked Done

Manufacturing Run and Biomass Request

mfg_outputbiomass_requesttransform / transfer

A Manufacturing Run packs input biomass into units (pre-rolls, flower packaging, concentrates). A Biomass Request moves biomass from Processing to Manufacturing: requested → approved → transferred.

Manufacturing Run

packedLb     = units × unit size g ÷ 454
packYieldPct = packedLb ÷ inputLb

Biomass Request

  • Category, strain, requested lbs; source UID once approved
  • Only transferredLb moves stock, and only when status is transferred
  • Flagged when still "requested" after 3 days

Shipping material leaves

Product Transfer — Outgoing

transfer_outMetrc Queue

The paper "Product Transfer Form (OUTGOING)" v7. Material often ships as is, without becoming finished goods: on-stem flower back to the farm customer, or bucked flower, flower, smalls and trim to a buyer. The product type code says which Raw Inventory tab it comes off. Weights entered on a Hand Trim that's still open count as stock right away, so product picked up before the trim form is submitted can still go on a transfer.

On the form

  • Customer, dates, driver, plate
  • Per package: type 1–9, then the batch (the on-hand packages on that type's Raw Inventory tab — fills strain, PID, CID, source UID and shows what's available), weight (units for pre-rolls and pouches), transfer UID, $/lb
  • Manifest #, their license, destination, sales order # and manifest initiated / accepted come from Canix onto the record (Metrc Queue, Transfers report); invoice #, customer order #, terms and the office checklist only with Fix form

Math

totalLb     = Σ weight  (pre-rolls left out)
totalPieces = Σ weight of type 8 rows
totalValue  = Σ (weight × price per lb)

Type → comes off

  • 1 Unbucked → On-Stem · 2 Bucked → Bucked
  • 3 Trimmed flower → Flower · 4 Smalls → Smalls
  • 5 Trim / biomass → Trim · 6 → Fresh Frozen
  • 8 Pre-rolls, 9 Ground flower pouches → Pre-Rolls & Pouches, in units (Adobe's packages in Canix, plus runs not in Metrc yet) · 7 Seeds: not tracked
  • A Metrc manifest from Adobe (CCL22-0001284) with no form shows as Needs form, prefilled from Canix

Receiving material comes back in

Product Transfer — Incoming

transfer_in

Dry or finished material that comes in from another facility without one of our forms having made it: a return (smalls sent back), product we trimmed at another site, a purchase. One form per manifest, a row per package. Each row becomes stock on the Raw Inventory tab its product type says, under the package's own UID, so the downstream forms and outgoing transfers take it off like any other batch. Wet whole plant still comes in on Wet Intake, and accepting the manifest is still the Metrc Queue's Incoming transfers list.

On the form

  • Person completing it, date received, why it's coming in (return / processed at another site / purchase / other), from (facility or customer), their DCC license #, Metrc manifest #, driver, plate
  • Per package: type 3–6, package UID (strain fills in from Canix), strain, trim type for trim, PID, CID, lbs, where it's stored

Math

totalLb = Σ weight
on hand = weight − what later forms took

Type → goes on

  • 3 Trimmed flower → Flower · 4 Smalls → Smalls
  • 5 Trim / biomass → Trim (with its trim type) · 6 → Fresh Frozen
  • Unbucked / bucked flower, seeds and pre-rolls aren't taken: Bucking and Hand Trim only work from our own queues
  • Source shows as "Incoming transfer", farm as the PID's farm, else the facility it came from

Rules that run through every form

454 grams per pound

Every grams → pounds conversion uses 454, the floor's round figure, not 453.592.

Four kinds of step

Origin adds weight (Harvest, Fresh Frozen). Moisture loses water on purpose (Take Down). Conserving should balance in = out (Bucking, trims). Transform turns biomass into finished goods (Pre-Roll, Pouches, Mfg Run).

The 5% balance check

On conserving steps, if (input − Σ outputs) ÷ input is more than 5% either way, the dashboard's Exceptions tab lists it as unbalanced.

One lot, many tags

Farm UID → Dry UID → release tags → new output tags are aliased back to one lot, so wet, dry, bucked and trimmed weights add up from farm to finished flower.

UIDs match by their ending

Two UIDs are the same package when one ends with the other, 4 characters or more. People can type the last digits.

How stock comes down

Raw Inventory has no table of its own. Stock comes off when a later form names its UID (its own new tag or the lot UID) and category, oldest first. Anything leaving without a form is marked Used up.

Metrc Queue

Every submitted form marked M above lands there as To do → In progress → Done / Not needed. New output tags are typed in there once the package exists in Metrc.

Labor

Hours come from Connecteam through the Labor Log and Crew Check-In, split over a project's UIDs by pounds, at each person's rate. Trimmers are paid finished pounds × the lot's rate.
